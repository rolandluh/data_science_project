Also in the data

  • Electric vehicle share, income, land use
  • Supermarkets, hospitals, universities
  • Substations as grid-connection proxy
  • Motorways and junctions

Limits

  • Usage data is biased toward larger networks
  • Usage exists only where stations already stand
  • Electric vehicle share and income are coarse (1–5 km), spread over 500 m
  • Private vs. public parking hard to tell apart in OpenStreetMap
  • No revenue data: returns rest on stated assumptions

Marko 5 · Optimization

DC or AC?

Context rule: the place decides the type

DC

  • Motorway services, rest area, junction
  • Primary road
  • Supermarket or discounter

AC

  • Everywhere else
  • Homes, offices, hospitals, P+R
  • Cars stand for hours
Money

Two budgets

  • One € budget for AC, one for DC
  • Never ranked against each other
Limitation: oversimplistic. Addressed in a future version.
Marko 5 · Optimization

Greedy search: pick until the money runs out

Filter

Only spots in the chosen radius

Rank

By predicted point-hours, separately for AC and DC

Space

Skip spots too close to an earlier pick

Pay

Site cost = per-site + per-point cost × points; stop at budget

Limits
  • Busiest sites first, not most usage per euro
  • Hard spacing cutoff
  • No guaranteed optimum

Deliberately primitive for now. The priority is getting the demand model right; a better optimizer follows easily from it.

Marko 5 · Optimization

Sizing algorithm

1

Predict demand for a 2-point site

2

Spread it over 2, 4, 6 … points

3

Look up how often such sites are full

4

Pick the smallest size that fits the rule

AC fullness rule

≤ 31 % of days full

DC fullness rule

≤ 5 % of days full

Full day: in its busiest hour, every charging point was in use for the whole hour.

Marko 6 · Limitations

Five things that can mislead

1The feed sees about half of public pointsMostly big networks and fast charging
2Occupancy is not demandOvernight AC looks busy; AC and DC not comparable
3Price is missingThe strongest driver of usage
4Picks sit in gapsWhere the model is weakest; the flag shows it
5The optimizer is still primitiveGreedy, hard spacing cutoff, unvalidated
