Caveats

Known Limitations

  • Temporal mismatch: KBA vehicle data (Oct 2024) lags ~2 years behind charging data (Sep 2026). EV adoption has likely grown.
  • Boundary edition: VG250 is from 2020; municipal mergers since may cause join mismatches.
  • Historic coverage bias: MobiData BW feed favors networks with live feeds; smaller operators may be underrepresented.
  • Street parking completeness: ON_STREET data comes from specific municipal surveys, not a comprehensive BW-wide census.
  • Not yet integrated: KBA FZ 13 (vehicle classes), KBA 5 km EV grid (fine-grained EV share).
Methods

Planned Analysis Methods

Spatial Analysis

  • Gap analysis: Overlay charger density vs demand proxies (population, EV share) per district to find underserved areas
  • Clustering: DBSCAN or k-means on charger locations to identify coverage clusters and spatial gaps
  • Spatial autocorrelation: Moran’s I to test whether charger placement correlates with demand or is spatially random

Scoring & Utilization

  • Weighted scoring model: Rank candidate locations (parking sites without nearby chargers) by population density + EV share + distance to nearest charger
  • Historic utilization: Use MobiData availability snapshots to identify high-demand stations (low availability = high usage), then characterize what makes those locations attractive
All methods use scikit-learn, geopandas, and scipy — already in the project stack. Results feed into the Leaflet map as a recommendation layer.

Placement Optimization Pipeline

Input: k stations to build. k = Budget ÷ Price-per-CS-Type (prices scraped & averaged, updated monthly).

Level 0 — Baseline & Benchmark

ComponentDescription
A0Demand grid5 km grid with EV-share × Population. Superimpose existing CS.
B0Greedy max-coverageRadius R around existing CS = covered. Max Σ demand × covered, s.t. ≤k new sites.
CEvaluationCompare against recent installations as ground truth.

Level 1 — Exact Optimization

ComponentDescription
A1Better demandAdd weights: house/apartment density, POI proximity, literature-backed factors.
B1Exact solverReplace greedy with HiGHS / OR-Tools / PuLP (open-source mathematical optimization).

Level 2 — ML-based

ComponentDescription
A2ML demandXGBoost / Gradient Boosting. Features: population, EV density, home-charging gap, POIs, road class, traffic, station attrs, local context.
B2Spatial placementSpatial search models with competition & road-distance. Needs further research.
Limitations: endogeneity & selection bias — stations placed because demand exists biases training data.
